# Changelog

Plain-English notes for Mom. The newest entry goes at the top; the app's update banner shows it.
Write what she'll notice, not how it was built.

## 0.3.0
- The app now tells you when a new version is ready. Press "Update now" and it restarts itself with the new version; "Later" hides the note.
- There's a "Print Explosion 3D" app for your Applications folder and Dock.

## 0.2.0
- You can chat with the designer now: describe what you want, or share a photo (drop it in, paste it, or press the camera).
- Your design shows up and spins on the right. Change sizes and options on the far right, then press "Update the model".
- Save for printing, or open it straight in Bambu Studio.
- Start a new project, switch between projects, or delete one from the top of the chat.

## 0.1.0
- The app has its own name and icon now.
