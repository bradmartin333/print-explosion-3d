      How this works

      Making something

      Tell me what you'd like in the chat on the left, or share a photo of something to copy. Your design appears in the middle and slowly turns. Drag it to look from every side, or click it to stop it turning.

      Changing it

      The boxes and sliders on the right change sizes and options. Change as many as you like, then press Update the model. You can also just ask me in the chat, like “make it a bit taller”.

      Went the wrong way?

      Press Go back one step, or type “undo” in the chat. You can press it again to keep going back.

      Is it ready to print?

      The card in the corner of the preview tells you how big it is, about how long it will take, and warns you if it might be tricky to print. Tick Show the print plate to see it next to the printer's plate.

      Printing it

      Open in Bambu Studio sends it straight to your printer's app. Save for printing saves a file instead. Change Copies to print to fit several on one plate, or type “print 6 of these”.

      Labels

      Say “make some labels”. Type the words you want, separated by commas, in the box on the right, then press Update the model.

      Your projects

      Everything saves by itself. Click the project name at the top to switch, or type “open my bird feeder”. The ⋯ button can make a copy, or save a project as a file to send to someone else (and open one they sent you). “Delete” moves a project to the Trash, so you can still get it back.

      Choosing the AI helper

      In the ⋯ menu, AI helper picks which AI does the designing. “Everyday” is right for almost everything. Pick “Extra careful” for a tricky idea. It's slower and costs more. Your next message starts a fresh conversation, and your design carries over.

      Making things easier to see

      The ⋯ button also has bigger text, stronger colors, and a way to stop the model turning.

      If something goes wrong

      Try again, or describe it a little differently. Your work is saved, so nothing is lost. If it keeps happening, ask whoever set this up to take a look.
